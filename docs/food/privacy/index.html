Privacy

Privacy policy. Last updated 17 November 2026.

Cooked it, Ate it is a private family recipe app. It is shared by invitation with family members only. This page explains in plain words what the app keeps and who can see it.

What you share

Only what you choose to add:

  • The name you give the app, and an optional picture (a photo, a selfie, an emoji or a sticker).
  • Recipes, photos, videos and comments you post.

This is stored in the app's iCloud database so the rest of the family can see it. Anyone who has been invited to the app can see what is shared there.

What stays on your device

Your favourites, your private notes on recipes and your colour choice stay on your own phone or iPad. They are not shared.

What the app does not do

  • No adverts and no tracking.
  • No analytics or third-party tools.
  • No accounts or passwords.
  • Your information is never sold or given to anyone for marketing.

Camera and photos

The app asks to use your camera or photo library only when you choose to take a selfie, or add a photo or video. It does not look at them at any other time.

Deleting things

You can delete your own recipes, posts and comments inside the app, and change or remove your picture. To have everything you added removed, get in touch using the address below.

Children

The app is meant for a family, including children using it with a parent or carer. It does not ask anyone for personal details beyond the name they choose to type.
