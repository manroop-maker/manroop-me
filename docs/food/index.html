Cooked it, Ate it

Passed down, cooked together, eaten quickly

Our family's recipes, kept safe and kept alive. Handwritten cards, old cookbook pages and the dishes we grew up on, all in one place, for the whole family.

What it does

  • Read each recipe typed up clearly, then see the original handwritten card, just for nostalgia.
  • Punjabi recipes in both English and Punjabi.
  • See whose kitchen each recipe came from.
  • Post photos and videos when you cook it, add your own recipes and leave comments.
  • Pick your own colours and your own picture.

Who can get it

It is a private app for our family, and it is not on the App Store. If you are family, ask Manroop to add your phone and give you the password. Then open this page in Safari on your iPhone or iPad and tap the button.
