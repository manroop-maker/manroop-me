Our Family Tree

Standing on the shoulders of those who came before

Our family's story, kept in one place. The generations we know from old charts, the Haridwar registers and the memories of our elders, laid out as a living tree for the whole family to explore.

What it does

  • Pinch and zoom through the generations, on iPhone and iPad.
  • Tap anyone to see their card: parents, children, brothers and sisters, and where each detail comes from.
  • Short video clips of our elders telling the family's stories, with the words written underneath.
  • Our family in history: a timeline from the misls to Partition to coming to England.
  • Show it on the TV with AirPlay.

Who can get it

It is a private app for our family only. It is not on the App Store. If you are family and would like it on your iPhone or iPad, ask Manroop to add your device and give you the password. Then open this page in Safari and tap the button.
