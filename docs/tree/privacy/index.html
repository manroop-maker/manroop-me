Privacy

Privacy policy. Last updated 20 November 2026.

Our Family Tree is a private app for one family. It is shared by invitation with family members only. This page explains in plain words what the app holds and who can see it.

What is in the app

The family tree itself: names, rough dates, relationships, family stories, a few old photos and short video clips of elders talking about the family. All of it comes from the family and is built into the app.

Who can see it

Only family members who have been given the app. It is not on the App Store and there is no public website showing the tree.

What the app does not do

  • It does not collect anything from you. There is nothing to sign up for and no account or password.
  • No adverts, no tracking, no analytics and no third-party tools.
  • It does not use the internet to show the tree. Everything works offline.
  • Your information is never sold or given to anyone.

Living relatives

For people who are alive, the app keeps details to what the family is happy to share. If you would like something about you changed or removed, get in touch and it will be done in the next version.
